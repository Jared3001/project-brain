Across The Meter™ Power

engineered for full autonomy

Power Stack

Joulent’s custom Across the Meter™ power stack manages all-of-the-above energy sources and grid interconnection to deliver reliable power at industrial scale.

Power Mix
We begin by deploying a geographically- and customer-optimized configuration of generation technologies, including both small and large turbines, fast-reacting long-duration battery storage, and renewables.
Across the Meter

By bringing generation to the grid, our projects unlock expedited load interconnection to supplement co-located power sources. BESS handles data center load transients and optimizes charge and discharge based on both the needs of the industrial customer and the opportunity to dispatch into and/or draw from the grid.

Orchestration

The Joulent stack orchestrates these behind-the-meter and front-of-the-meter resources so they can work in harmony across the meter, ensuring reliable and cost-effective power supply to the industrial load.

AI-Enhanced

Our customized power grid facilitates both micro and macro decision making, with a suite of local controls integrating into a central AI-enhanced “brain.”

Lower Cost

As a result, our customers receive the energy they need, when they need it, at a lower cost of compute.
