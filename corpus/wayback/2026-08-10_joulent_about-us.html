Alan McGinnis
Technical Program Manager

Alan holds a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Missouri, an M.S. in Architectural Engineering from the University of Kansas, and an MBA from Duke University’s Fuqua School of Business. He continues to serve as an officer in the U.S. Navy Reserve.
