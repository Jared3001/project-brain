Each claim is read with a pattern anchored on its document's wording, or taken from a fact the record already verified, and its quote is checked against the cited page. Verdicts come from plain rules: numbers more than 3% apart disagree; dates compare by year, and by day where a source gives one; statements that change after a dated event read as a change, not a contradiction. Press is quoted one line at a time and linked to the original.
